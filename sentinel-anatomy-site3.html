02 — The problem

Security systems don't talk to each other.

Access systems know whether a credential succeeded or failed. Gas sensors alarm on their own. Environmental readings sit in isolation. After something happens, someone has to piece it together by hand.

  • “Who approached?”
  • “Was the credential authorized?”
  • “Were repeated access attempts made?”
  • “What changed in the environment?”
  • “Why did the system trigger?”

SENTINEL connects those events into one system.
